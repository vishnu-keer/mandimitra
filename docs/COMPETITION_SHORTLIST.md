## Recommendation

1. **Ask VGU's SIH coordinator (SPOC) this week** whether a team can still be
   nominated for SIH 2026 or its next internal round. If yes, the
   agriculture theme fits; you will need a 6-person team.
2. **Prepare for the next Pune Agri Hackathon** now. Its rules ask for at
   most 10 slides and a 1–2 minute video: the 12-slide deck needs two slides
   cut (slides 5 and 9 are the easiest to merge), and the storyboard in
   `docs/DEMO_VIDEO_STORYBOARD.md` is already 2 minutes.
3. Watch the Imagine Cup 2027 rules when published; entering would require
   building on Microsoft AI services, which is a scope change.
4. Re-check event.data.gov.in and AIKosh monthly for open-data and
   agriculture challenges.

No registration fee has been paid or recommended. Do not register for any
paid event without reviewing the fee first.
