# User research plan

Status: **not started.** No participant has been contacted.

## Goal

Learn whether farmers and traders read MandiMitra's screens correctly and
whether any of them would use it, before claiming any benefit.

## Participants

5–10 adults at a Jaipur mandi or through a farmer producer organisation
(FPO): a mix of commission agents, traders and farmers. Recruited through an
introduction (college, mandi committee or FPO), never cold-approached while
working at a busy time.

## Consent

- Explain in Hindi or English, before starting: who we are, the purpose,
  that it takes about 15 minutes, that participation is voluntary, that they
  can stop at any time, and that no names or phone numbers are recorded.
- Ask for verbal consent; note "consent given" and the date only.
- No photos, audio or video unless separately agreed; never of faces.
- No payment is offered (₹0 budget); thank participants.

## Session (≈15 minutes, on a phone or laptop)

1. Background (no identifiers): role (farmer/trader/agent), commodities,
   how they currently learn prices.
2. Show the Overview in their preferred language. Ask:
   - "What does this screen tell you?"
   - "Is this price for today?" (O1)
   - "What does 'same as today' mean here?" (O2)
3. Show the Forecast tab. Ask: "How sure is this forecast? How can you tell?" (O3)
4. "Would anything here change what you sell or buy? Which part?" (O4)
5. "Would you act on this forecast alone?" (O5)
6. "What is missing that you would need?"

## Recording

A paper or spreadsheet form per session with: session number, date, role,
commodity, answers in short notes, consent given (yes). No names, phone
numbers, villages or photos. Store on the researcher's laptop; delete raw
notes after the anonymised summary is written.

## Analysis

Count correct answers for O1–O3 and O5; group O4 and "what is missing" answers
into themes; report all of it, including negative findings.

## Ethics

Check whether Vivekananda Global University requires approval for
minimal-risk interviews before starting. Do not give price advice during the
session.
