**Definitions.**
- *Zero price*: the source records `0` where no price was reported. These are treated as missing, never as a price of zero.
- *Days with a real price*: calendar days on which the mandi reported a non-zero price.
- *Carried forward %*: share of feature rows whose `price_t` was carried forward from an earlier reported day (at most 2 days). These rows are inputs only.
- *Usable h=k*: rows with a complete feature vector **and** a genuinely reported target price k days later. Only these are ever trained on or scored. Carried-forward targets are never scored.
- *Gate*: a pair is evaluated only if it has at least 400 usable rows. The gate was set before any of these markets were examined and was not changed afterwards.

## Known data limitations

- Historical only: the source ends on 28 April 2015.
- One price per centre per day (modal); no min/max, so the `spread_pct` feature is all-empty and is dropped automatically.
- No arrivals, weather, MSP, festival or transport data.
- Jodhpur and Kota report far less often than Jaipur and do not clear the gate; they are shown as excluded, not forecast.
